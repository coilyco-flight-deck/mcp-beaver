The mark is the content

Dropping the {{.Undeclared}} silent servers would keep the index safe and lose the most interesting thing in it. They stay, carrying annotation-coverage undeclared, which a consumer can filter on rather than parsing comments.

Safety is unchanged by the mark. A silent tool is simply not in the allowlist, and the allowlist is closed by construction: anything absent is denied without being named. Nothing is exposed on the strength of a guess.

There are no withhold blocks. withhold mints a discoverable stub that appears in tools/list, so one per excluded tool would advertise a surface that mostly refuses, paid for by the consumer on every turn. The allowlist is closed by construction, so a denial list enforces nothing and only goes stale. withhold stays a hand-authoring device.

No schemas, deliberately. The proxy snapshots the upstream contracts at connect time and fails closed on drift, so restating them in KDL would duplicate a source of truth that rots. The file carries policy.

What this run does not settle

readOnlyHint is self-declared. An upstream that mislabels is trusted here. Nothing in this pipeline calls a tool to find out, and nothing should.

One reading, one day. Surfaces move. A directory that shows drift needs the run on a schedule and a history, and this page is one run.
